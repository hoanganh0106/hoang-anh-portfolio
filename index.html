Medical devices that think on the spot, for clinics without specialists or internet.

Edge AI for Health is a student-led project by Hoang Anh Nguyen at Hanoi University of Science and Technology. I design low-cost devices that run AI on the chip itself, so they work offline and patient data never leaves the device.

Why this project

A good examination shouldn’t depend on where you live.

  1. 01

    Few specialists at the front line

    A commune health station is often a patient’s first and only stop, yet heart and lung sounds take years of practice to judge well.

  2. 02

    Cloud AI doesn’t reach the village

    Many smart tools stop working when the connection drops — exactly in the places that need them most.

  3. 03

    Imported devices cost too much

    Each device uses common, affordable parts so a health station could own one, repair it, and build another.

Design principles

Four rules every device follows.

  • Works offline

    The AI runs on the device’s own chip. No signal, no problem.

  • Data stays on the device

    Recordings are processed and kept locally, never uploaded by default.

  • Affordable and repairable

    Off-the-shelf parts that a local technician can source and replace.

  • Open to the community

    Designs and code are shared as each device reaches a safe, documented stage.

How it works

Every device follows the same four steps — all of them on the device.

  1. 1

    Sense

    Sound, heart rhythm or a hearing response, captured by low-cost sensors.

  2. 2

    Clean

    Separate overlapping sources and remove the noise of a real clinic.

  3. 3

    Decide on the chip

    A small model, sized for a microcontroller, highlights what needs attention.

  4. 4

    Show it here

    A clear result on the device. The clinician makes the call.

Research foundation

Built on speech-separation research.

Telling apart several sounds that overlap — three voices at once, a heartbeat under a breath, a cough in a noisy room — is the same problem. My separation research is the core of the acoustic devices.
