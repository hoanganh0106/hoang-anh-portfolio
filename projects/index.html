Devices & work

Four devices, one goal: good screening anywhere.

Each device is labelled with its real stage. Below them is the research they are built on and the earlier work that led here.

Devices

Working prototype

Edge AI Stethoscope

Separates heart and lung sounds, filters noise and flags patterns worth a second listen — entirely on the device, with no internet.

How it works →
In development

Cough Listener

A small box for clinic waiting rooms that picks out coughs from background noise and suggests who may need TB or pneumonia screening.

How it works →
In development

Single-lead ECG Patch

A thin chest patch worn for several days that watches for irregular heart rhythms such as atrial fibrillation and saves only the moments that matter.

How it works →
In development

School Hearing Screener

A headphone kit that lets a school nurse run a quick hearing check, with a built-in microphone that pauses the test when the room is too loud.
